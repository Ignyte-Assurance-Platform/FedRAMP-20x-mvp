# ADR quality checklist

- Context states the forces (constraints, deadlines, people, costs) and the date.
- Decision is one paragraph, present tense, no hedging.
- Consequences list what becomes easier AND what becomes harder.
- At least two alternatives with the reason they lost.
- Links: the work item or meeting that triggered it; the architecture view it affects; the ADR it supersedes (if any).
- `deciders:` names real people/roles. An agent is never a decider.
